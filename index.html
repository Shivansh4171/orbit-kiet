What is Orbit?

Orbit is an unofficial student-made companion for your college's CyberVidya portal. It does not replace CyberVidya or ask you to create another account. After you log in to CyberVidya normally, Orbit reads the attendance data already available to your session and presents it in a much cleaner way — including overall and subject-wise attendance, a 75% attendance planner, lecture history, today's classes and the next four days of timetable.

5-step written tutorial

Follow these steps in order. The important part is to load your CyberVidya Dashboard once before opening Orbit.

01

Download Orbit

Choose the PC build for a desktop Chromium browser or the Quetta build for Android.

02

Install the extension

Load the downloaded ZIP through your browser's extension workflow. The exact process is covered in the video guides below.

03

Log in to CyberVidya

Open CyberVidya and log in normally using your college credentials. Orbit does not need your password separately.

04

Load the Dashboard

Open the CyberVidya Dashboard and wait for it to load completely at least once. This initializes the session Orbit needs.

05

Refresh Orbit

Open or refresh Orbit after the Dashboard has loaded. Your attendance, planner, history and timetable should now appear.

Important: Before opening Orbit, log in to CyberVidya and load the Dashboard completely at least once. If Orbit shows a red error, refresh Orbit after the Dashboard has loaded.

Frequently asked questions
Orbit is showing a red error. Is it broken?
Usually, no. First make sure you are logged into CyberVidya and have loaded the Dashboard at least once. Then refresh Orbit. Orbit reads data from your authenticated CyberVidya session, so it cannot work before that session is ready.
Why do I need to load the Dashboard first?
CyberVidya initializes the authenticated session and the required portal data while the Dashboard loads. Orbit uses that existing session rather than asking you for your CyberVidya credentials.
Does Orbit store my CyberVidya password?
No. Orbit does not need your CyberVidya password to display the attendance data. Log in directly through the official CyberVidya portal.
Which browsers are supported?
The PC build is intended for Chromium-based desktop browsers such as Chrome, Brave and Edge. The mobile build is prepared for Quetta Browser on Android.
Where can I learn how to install Orbit?
You can follow the 5-step written tutorial above or watch the separate PC and Quetta video tutorials.
Is Orbit an official CyberVidya or college product?
No. Orbit is an unofficial, student-made companion. CyberVidya remains the official attendance portal.
